# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is welcome when it focuses on evidence and the work.
Review the change, not the person. Assume good intent, correct mistakes openly,
and make space for people with different experience levels and accessibility
needs.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
spaces where someone represents the AI Reader project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation requests, open an issue without including sensitive
personal details. For a confidential report, use the repository Security tab's
private **Report a vulnerability** channel and label the submission as a code
of conduct report.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports
will be reviewed as privately and impartially as the available GitHub tools
allow. Retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.
